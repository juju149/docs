# Fans

**Fans** are subscribers who interact with your content. Elys tracks their engagement, spending, and provides AI-powered insights.

## Fan Structure

```json
{
  "id": "fan_123",
  "modelId": "model_abc",
  "platform": "ONLYFANS",
  "externalId": "of_user_456",
  "username": "mike_loves_luna",
  "displayName": "Mike",
  "avatar": "https://...",
  "totalSpent": 250.00,
  "isSubscribed": true,
  "subscriptionTier": "VIP",
  "subscribedAt": "2025-12-01T00:00:00Z",
  "expiresAt": "2026-02-01T00:00:00Z",
  "aiScore": 85,
  "tags": [...]
}
```

## AI Score

The **AI Score** (0-100) indicates fan engagement potential:

| Score | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 0-30 | Cold 🥶 | Low engagement, unlikely to purchase |
| 31-50 | Warm 🌡️ | Moderate interest, needs nurturing |
| 51-70 | Hot 🔥 | High engagement, ready to buy |
| 71-90 | Whale 🐋 | Top spender, VIP treatment |
| 91-100 | Superfan 💎 | Maximum value, exclusive content |

## Platforms

Elys supports fans from:

- **ONLYFANS** - OnlyFans subscribers
- **FANSLY** - Fansly followers  
- **MYM** - MYM subscribers
