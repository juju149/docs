# Welcome to Elys API

Elys is an **AI-powered content creator management platform** designed for OnlyFans, Fansly, and MYM creators who want to streamline their fan engagement and maximize revenue.

## Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Messaging
Generate personalized responses that match your model's unique personality, writing style, and tone. The AI learns from your persona configuration to create authentic interactions.

### 💰 Revenue Optimization
Track fan spending patterns, identify high-value fans (whales), and optimize your engagement strategy for maximum revenue.

### 📊 Unified Dashboard
Manage multiple platforms (OnlyFans, Fansly, MYM) from a single interface with consolidated analytics.

### 🔒 Secure & Private
All data is encrypted and stored securely. Your conversations and fan data never leave your control.
